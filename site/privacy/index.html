隐私说明Privacy information

你的壁纸只属于你Your wallpapers stay yours

AdaptiveWallpaper 是一款离线 Android 应用，不申请网络权限，也不包含广告或分析 SDK。 AdaptiveWallpaper is an offline Android app. It requests no network permission and contains no advertising or analytics SDK.

我们不会收集什么What we do not collect

应用不会收集、上传、出售或分享你的图片、设备标识、使用记录或个人信息。应用没有账户系统、后端服务或远程数据库。

The app does not collect, upload, sell, or share your images, device identifiers, usage history, or personal information. It has no account system, backend service, or remote database.

图片如何保存How images are stored

你选择的浅色与深色壁纸会复制到应用的私有存储中，其他应用无法直接读取。这些文件被排除在 Android 云备份之外。

Your selected wallpapers are copied into private app storage that other apps cannot directly read. These files are excluded from Android cloud backups.

如何删除How to delete data

你可以在应用中重新选择图片。卸载 AdaptiveWallpaper 会由 Android 删除其私有文件；卸载前建议先在应用中停用动态壁纸。

You can replace either image inside the app. Android removes the app's private files when you uninstall AdaptiveWallpaper; disable the live wallpaper in the app first.
